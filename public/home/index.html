      Questions

      How do I get Latent?

      Join Early Access. We’re letting people in in waves, and we’ll email you when your spot opens.

      Is Latent free?

      Yes. Latent is free.

      Does my camera have to be on?

      No. Latent also works with the camera off, and the same session rules apply: switch to another app and the clock stops, so your phone still can’t distract you. You can even lock your phone and the timer keeps going.

      Can Latent block distracting apps?

      Yes. Pick the apps that pull you away, and Latent blocks them while you record.

      What if I need a break?

      Pause whenever you need to. Your session waits 15 minutes for you to come back. Or record in Pomodoro rounds, with the breaks built in.

      Who can see my sessions?

      You choose for each session: keep it to yourself, share it with friends, or post it to a group.

      Do I need friends on Latent?

      No. Your sessions, stats and streak all work on your own. Friends are there when you want the extra push.

      Is there an Android or iPad app?

      Not yet. Latent is made for iPhone.
